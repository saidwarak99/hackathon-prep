Restaurant: Test Kitchen 47
Document type: Fictional restaurant SOP
Document ID: RAG-SOP-TEST-001
Opening procedure:
The restaurant opens at 10:00 AM.
The kitchen temperature log must be checked before food preparation.
The opening supervisor must record the temperature check.
Closing procedure:
The restaurant closes at 10:00 PM.
The closing supervisor must reconcile the cash register.
All refrigerators must be checked and recorded before staff leave.
Food safety:
Staff must record refrigerator temperatures at opening and closing.
Any temperature outside the restaurant's approved safety limits must be escalated to the supervisor.
This test document does not define a numeric safe-temperature threshold.
