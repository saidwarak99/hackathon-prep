CAFE OPERATIONS SOP

MILK STORAGE POLICY

Whole milk must be stored below 4°C.

Opened milk cartons should be used within 3 days.

Milk past its expiry date must not be used.

During peak hours, staff should check milk inventory every 2 hours.

INVENTORY POLICY

Inventory should be checked at the beginning and end of every shift.

Items below their reorder threshold should be flagged for restocking.

Low-stock items should be reported to the cafe manager.

FOOD SAFETY POLICY

Employees must wash their hands before handling food.

Cross-contamination between raw and ready-to-eat food must be prevented.

Expired food must never be served to customers.

CUSTOMER COMPLAINT POLICY

Customer complaints should be acknowledged politely.

Staff should attempt to resolve simple complaints immediately.

Serious complaints should be escalated to the cafe manager.
